**Emergency at sea: VHF channel 16.** Greek coastguard is 108.

## Money

Marina fees and fuel are in the charter. Tavernas, drinks ashore and the crew tip
are not. Cash is easier on the smaller islands — Kythnos has one cash machine
that is often empty.

## Connectivity

There is no boat WiFi. Greek mobile coverage is good near the islands and
disappears mid-passage. Do not plan a call for a passage day.

## Dietary requirements

Provisioning was done to the list collected before departure. Noor eats no
shellfish and the galley is briefed. Tavernas will be told on arrival.
