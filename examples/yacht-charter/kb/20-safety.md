# Safety

The briefing on the first evening is mandatory for everyone, including anyone who
has sailed before.

- **Lifejackets** are under the cockpit seats. Wear one at night, in any swell,
  and whenever the skipper says.
- **One hand for the boat.** Always hold something when moving on deck.
- **Man overboard** — shout, point continuously, and do not stop pointing.
- **Boom** — it moves fast and without warning during a gybe. Listen for the call.
- **Swimming** only when the engine is off and the skipper has said so.

The skipper's word is final on anything related to safety or weather. That is not
a formality.
