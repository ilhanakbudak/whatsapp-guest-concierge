# The boat

*Meltemi* is a 50ft monohull, four double cabins with ensuites plus a skipper's
cabin.

## Packing

**Soft bags only.** There is no space to stow a hard suitcase; the crew will have
to store it ashore at your cost.

Bring deck shoes with pale soles, a windproof layer for evenings, and more sun
protection than you think. Nothing dries quickly at sea.

## Water and power

Water is limited between marinas. Showers are short — the crew will say when
tanks are low. Power for charging is available under way and in marina berths;
avoid hairdryers.

## Heads

Nothing goes into the heads that has not been eaten first. Blockages at sea are
genuinely serious.
