# Logistics

**Hotel** — Avenida da Liberdade 180, Lisbon 1250-146

**From the airport** — 20 minutes by taxi, around €15. Uber and Bolt both work.
Keep the receipt; airport transfers are reimbursable.

**Sessions** are in the Atlantic room on the first floor unless the schedule says
otherwise. The Douro room is the second breakout space, along the same corridor.

**Coaches to the airport** leave the hotel front at 12:00 and 15:00 on the final
day. Message this number to change which one you are on.
