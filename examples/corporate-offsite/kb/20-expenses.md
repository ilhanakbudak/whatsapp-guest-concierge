# Expenses and what is covered

## Covered

- Accommodation, booked centrally — nothing to pay at checkout except extras
- All scheduled meals
- Airport transfers at both ends, including taxis
- One checked bag if your fare did not include it

## Not covered

- Minibar, room service and spa
- Drinks outside the scheduled dinners
- Anything for a partner or guest travelling with you

## Submitting

Photograph receipts and submit through the usual expenses tool within two weeks.
Anything above €75 needs a line of explanation.
