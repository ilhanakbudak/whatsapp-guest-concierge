# The weekend

## Friday
Arrivals from 16:00. Welcome drinks at 19:00 in the orangery, informal supper
from 20:30.

## Saturday
Breakfast 09:00–10:30. **Be seated on the chapel lawn by 13:15** — the ceremony
starts at 13:30 and the doors close. Drinks and photographs follow, wedding
breakfast at 16:30, first dance at 20:00.

## Sunday
Farewell brunch 09:30–11:30. Rooms to be vacated by 11:00.
