# Getting here and parking

**Thornbury Hall**, Thornbury Lane, Nr. Marlborough, Wiltshire SN8 4TH

**Driving.** The gates are on Thornbury Lane, not the main road — satnav often
sends people to the farm entrance a quarter of a mile earlier. If you reach the
white cottage you have gone too far. Parking is in the paddock, signposted, and
cars may be left overnight.

**Train.** Pewsey is the nearest station, 20 minutes by taxi. Book taxis ahead —
there is rarely a rank.

**Coaches.** A coach runs from the White Hart in Marlborough at 12:15 on the
Saturday and returns at 23:30 and 00:30.
