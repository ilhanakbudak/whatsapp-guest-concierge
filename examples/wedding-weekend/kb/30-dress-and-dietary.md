# Dress code and food

## Dress code

**Friday evening** — smart casual. It gets cold on the terrace after dark.

**Saturday** — morning suits and day dresses. The chapel lawn is grass, so
stiletto heels are a mistake many people make; block heels are wiser.

**Sunday brunch** — anything comfortable.

## Dietary requirements

All requirements collected with the RSVPs have been passed to the caterers.
Vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options are available at every meal. Amara is
coeliac and the kitchen is briefed on cross-contamination.

If something was missed, tell this number and it will reach Nadia.
