# The chalet

**Chalet Edelweiss**, Dorfstrasse 44, 3906 Saas-Fee

## Boot room

On the lower floor, heated. **Ski boots do not go upstairs** — the floors upstairs
are soft wood and mark permanently. Boot warmers are on overnight; put boots on
the pegs, not the floor.

## Meals

| | |
|---|---|
| Breakfast | 07:45–09:00 |
| Afternoon tea | 16:30 |
| Dinner | 19:30 |

Dinner is served together at one table. If you will miss it, tell this number by
16:00 so the kitchen knows.

On Ana's day off, breakfast is laid out cold and there is no dinner. Village
restaurant suggestions are in the dining section.

## Heating and the drying room

Underfloor heating is set centrally — please do not adjust the thermostats.
The drying room next to the boot room takes gloves and base layers overnight.
