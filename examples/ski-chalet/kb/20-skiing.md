# Skiing

## Lift passes

Six-day passes are in the chalet safe; Ana hands them out at the welcome dinner.
A lost pass costs CHF 40 to replace at the lift office — report it the same day.

## The minibus

Leaves the chalet at **09:15** for the gondola and does a return run at 09:45.
Afternoon pickups are at 16:00 and 16:45 from the gondola base. It leaves
promptly; it will not wait.

## Lessons

Fenella's beginner lessons are booked for the first three mornings, 10:00–12:00,
meeting at the nursery slope board.

## Conditions

Ask this number for today's conditions and it will tell you what the schedule
says. For live avalanche risk always check the board at the gondola base — that
is the authority, not this bot.

## Equipment

Fitting is at Sport Bruggmann in the village centre, booked under the chalet name.
Anything broken can be swapped there through the week at no cost.
