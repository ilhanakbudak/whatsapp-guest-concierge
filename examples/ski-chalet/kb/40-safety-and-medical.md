# Safety and medical

## Emergencies

**Europe-wide emergency number: 112.** Mountain rescue: 1414 (Rega).

Give your location as precisely as you can — the piste number and the nearest
lift is far more useful than a place name.

## Medical

The village clinic is by the tourist office, open 08:00–18:00. For anything
serious the hospital is in Visp, 40 minutes by road.

Diego carries an EpiPen for a nut allergy. The kitchen is briefed and every
mountain restaurant booking has been told.

## Insurance

Your travel insurance must cover winter sports and off-piste if you intend to ski
off-piste. Helicopter rescue in Switzerland is expensive and is not covered by an
EHIC or GHIC card.

## Off-piste

Do not go off-piste without a guide. Conditions here change quickly and the
terrain has real avalanche risk. Ana can book a guide with a day's notice.
