# Dining and the village

## On Ana's day off

- **Zur Mühle** — traditional, good rösti, book ahead
- **La Ferme** — fondue and raclette, very busy from 19:00
- **Pizzeria Boccalino** — casual, takes walk-ins, good with children

## On the mountain

- **Alpstubli**, mid-station — the group lunch booking is here
- **Gletschergrotte** — worth the detour, cash only

## Village practicalities

The Coop supermarket closes at 18:30 and does not open on Sunday. The pharmacy is
opposite the church. There is one cash machine, by the tourist office.

Saas-Fee is car-free — everything is walkable, and the electric taxis take about
five minutes end to end.
